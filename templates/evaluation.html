Understanding the Evaluation Metric

Mean Average Precision at 5 (mAP@5)

mAP@5 evaluates how effectively relevant medicinal leaf samples are ranked within the first five retrieval results. Average Precision at 5 is calculated for each query, and the average across queries produces mAP@5.

A higher score indicates better ranking performance on the evaluated split. It does not, by itself, establish performance on new photographs outside the dataset.
